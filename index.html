Wallet

Get rewarded for being active

Every account comes with a wallet. As you send messages you earn OneDollar Coins, and you can follow every credit and withdrawal in your transaction history.

  • Automatic rewards — coins are deposited as you reach messaging milestones.
  • Clear conversion — 1,000 Coins are worth $1.00 USD.
  • Withdraw your way — request a payout by PayPal, bank transfer or USDT (BEP-20) once you reach the $100 minimum.
  • Reviewed by admins — each withdrawal request is checked and approved by the DLS team.

FAQ

Common questions

Is DLS Chat free to use?

Yes. Creating an account, messaging and joining communities are all free.

What are OneDollar Coins?

They're the in-app reward currency. You earn them automatically by sending messages, and 1,000 Coins are worth $1.00 USD.

How do I withdraw?

Open your wallet, tap Withdraw, choose PayPal, bank transfer or USDT (BEP-20), and enter your payout details. The minimum withdrawal is $100 USD, and each request is reviewed by an admin before it is paid.

Who can post in announcement communities?

Only that community's admins. Members can read everything, which keeps important updates easy to find.

Can I create my own community?

Communities are created and run by the DLS team, so everything stays organised. Have an idea for one? Message an admin from the Community tab.

Which devices are supported?

DLS Chat runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, and follows your device's light or dark mode.
