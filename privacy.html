Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what information DLS Chat (“we”, “us”), operated by One Dollar Launch Shop, collects when you use our mobile app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — your full name, email address, phone number and password (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us).
  • Profile — an optional profile photo and bio.
  • Messages and media — the text, photos and videos you send, along with replies and reactions.
  • Payout details — when you request a withdrawal: your PayPal email, bank details (name, bank name, routing and account number) or USDT wallet address.

Information created as you use the app

  • Activity — the chats and communities you belong to, unread counts, and message delivery and read status.
  • Presence — whether you're online and when you were last seen.
  • Wallet records — your coin balance, reward transactions and withdrawal history.
  • Device information — if you turn on notifications, a push-notification token for your device.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, including email and phone verification.
  • To deliver your messages and media and show your profile to people you chat with.
  • To run communities, including showing public communities in Discover.
  • To calculate rewards, maintain your wallet and process withdrawal requests.
  • To enforce our community guidelines and terms, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To send you notifications about new messages and account activity, if you allow them.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other users can see your name, profile photo, bio and online status, and can find you by name, email or phone number.
  • Chat members can see the messages and media you send in that chat or community.
  • DLS admins can access account and wallet information to review withdrawals, moderate content and handle support.
  • Service providers — we use Google Firebase (authentication, database and file storage) to run the app. Payouts are sent through the payment method you choose (PayPal, your bank, or the BNB Smart Chain), which handle your details under their own policies.
  • Legal reasons — we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase and protected by access rules that limit each user to the chats and records they're allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. Wallet and withdrawal records may be kept longer where needed for accounting, fraud prevention or legal reasons. Messages you delete are removed from view for everyone in the conversation.

To delete your account and personal data, message an admin from the Community tab in the app and we'll process your request.

6. Your choices

  • Update your name, photo and bio at any time in your profile.
  • Turn off notifications and photo or camera access in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion.

7. Children

DLS Chat is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We don't knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll change the date at the top, and let you know in the app if the changes are significant.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Message an admin from the Community tab in DLS Chat, or visit Help & support.
