Community guidelines

Keep DLS Chat a great place.

These rules apply everywhere in the app — private chats, groups and communities. They're short on purpose. Breaking them can get your account suspended.

01

Be respectful

No harassment, bullying, threats or hate speech. Disagree with ideas, not people.

02

No spam or scams

Don't flood chats, send unwanted promotions, or try to trick people out of money, passwords or personal details.

03

Keep announcements clear

Announcement communities are for admins. Take discussion to a regular community or group chat.

04

Be real

Don't impersonate other people, the DLS team or other brands. One person, one account.

05

Play fair with rewards

Coins are for genuine conversation. Bots, scripts, fake accounts and message farming are not allowed.

06

Share responsibly

No illegal, sexually explicit or violent content, and don't share anyone's private information without their permission.

Enforcement

What happens if rules are broken

Community admins and the DLS team work together to keep things healthy.

  • Message removal — content that breaks the rules can be deleted for everyone.
  • Removal from communities — community admins can remove members from their community.
  • Account suspension — DLS admins can suspend accounts for serious or repeated violations, and any pending withdrawals may be rejected.
  • Getting help — if something's wrong, message an admin from the Community tab. See Help & support.

For community admins

If you've been made an admin

  • Lead by example — admins are held to these guidelines too.
  • Keep conversations on topic for the community's category and description.
  • Remove members only for real rule-breaking, not disagreements.
  • Escalate serious problems, like scams or threats, to the DLS team.
