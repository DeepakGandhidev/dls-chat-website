Rewards & wallet

Your activity, rewarded.

Every DLS Chat account includes a wallet. Stay active in your chats and communities, collect OneDollar Coins, and request a payout when you're ready.

OneDollar Coins

How coins work

OneDollar Coins are DLS Chat's in-app reward. They're added to your wallet automatically as you reach messaging milestones — there's nothing to claim.

  • Earned by chatting — send messages to other people to make progress toward your next reward.
  • Progress tracker — your wallet shows how close you are to the next deposit.
  • Full history — every reward and withdrawal is listed in your transactions.

Withdrawals

Cashing out, step by step

  1. 1

    Open your wallet

    Check your coin balance and its value in US dollars.

  2. 2

    Tap Withdraw

    Enter an amount of at least $100, up to your available balance.

  3. 3

    Choose a method

    PayPal, bank transfer or USDT on BEP-20, and add your payout details.

  4. 4

    Admin review

    The DLS team reviews the request and you'll see it approved or rejected in your history.

Good to know

Wallet rules

  • Coins are earned through genuine activity. Spam, fake accounts or automated messaging can lead to lost rewards and a suspended account — see the community guidelines.
  • Every withdrawal is reviewed manually, and a request can be rejected if the details are wrong or the activity breaks the rules.
  • Coins have no value outside DLS Chat and can't be transferred between users.
  • Reward amounts and withdrawal rules may change. The current details are always shown in the app.
