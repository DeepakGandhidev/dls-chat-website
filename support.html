Account & sign-in

How do I create an account?

Download DLS Chat, tap Register and enter your name, email, phone number and a password of at least 6 characters. We'll send a verification email — confirm it before you log in.

I didn't get my verification email.

Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure the email address you entered is correct. Then try logging in again.

Can I sign in with my phone number?

You can sign in with your phone number instead of email. We send a 6-digit code by SMS — enter it in the app to continue. If it doesn't arrive, you can request a new code.

I forgot my password.

On the login screen tap “Forgot Password?”, enter your email address, and follow the reset link we send you.

My account has been suspended.

Accounts are suspended when the community guidelines are broken. If you think it's a mistake, contact the DLS team.

Chats & communities

How do I start a chat?

Tap the new chat button, search for someone by name, email or phone, and open a conversation.

Can I delete a message?

Yes. Long-press a message you sent and choose Delete. It's removed for everyone in the conversation.

Why can't I post in a community?

It's probably an announcement-only community, where only admins can post. You can still read everything and react.

Can I create my own community?

Communities are created and run by the DLS team. If you have an idea for a new one, message an admin from the Community tab.

How do I leave a community?

Open the community, tap its name to see the info page, and choose Leave community.

Wallet & payouts

How do I earn coins?

Coins are added automatically as you send messages to other people. Your wallet shows your progress toward the next reward. Learn more on the Rewards page.

What's the minimum withdrawal?

$100 USD. At 1,000 Coins per dollar, that's 100,000 Coins.

How long does a withdrawal take?

Each request is reviewed manually by the DLS team. You can follow its status — pending, approved or rejected — in your withdrawal history.

Why was my withdrawal rejected?

Usually because the payout details were incomplete or incorrect, or the activity didn't follow the guidelines. Check your details and submit a new request, or contact the DLS team.

Still need a hand?

Open the Community tab in DLS Chat and message an admin — a real person will help you out.
