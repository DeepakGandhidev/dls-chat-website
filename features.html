Messaging

Chats that feel instant

Every message is delivered in real time. See when someone is typing, who's online, and when a message has been sent, delivered and read.

  • Direct messages — find anyone by name, email or phone number and start chatting.
  • Community chats — talk with everyone in the communities you've joined.
  • Replies — reply to a specific message so the context is always clear, even if the original is later removed.
  • Reactions — respond with an emoji without typing a word.
  • Delete for everyone — remove a message you sent from the whole conversation.
  • Search & unread counts — search your conversations and see at a glance what you've missed.

Announcements

Never miss what matters

Any community can be switched to announcement-only, so only its admins can post. The DLS team also runs official communities and can broadcast one announcement to several communities at once.

  • Highlighted posts — announcements stand out from regular messages.
  • Official badge — communities run by the DLS team are clearly marked.
